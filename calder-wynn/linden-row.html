About the house

Linden Row is a four-bedroom family house finished in white board-and-batten with black trim, set back from the street behind a planted front garden.

The ground floor is one open room: a kitchen with a long island, a dining table for eight and a living area that opens onto the deck. Upstairs, the main bedroom has its own bathroom and a dressing room; the other three bedrooms share a family bathroom.

The garden is level and fenced, with a timber deck off the kitchen and space for a play area at the back.
